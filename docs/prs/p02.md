# Phase 2 — Site editor rebuilt + shared UI kit

Base: `v2/p01-auth-rename` · Branch: `v2/p02-editor-ui`

The two 2,100-line operator pages are replaced by one `SiteEditor` that saves each section through the validated, versioned API. The couple dashboard (Phase 7) mounts the same editor with `mode="couple"`.

## What changed

**UI kit (`src/ui/`)** — Button, TextInput/TextArea/Select/Switch, BilingualField (English + বাংলা side by side), Card/CardHeader/CardBody, Badge + status badges, EmptyState, Toast (replaces `alert()`), ConfirmDialog (replaces `confirm()`). Built on new `sb-*` brand tokens in `globals.css` (paper, ink, marigold, holud, alta). Bangla inputs use Hind Siliguri (Galada stays a display face).

**Site editor (`src/components/editor/`)**
- `SiteEditor` — grouped section nav (Basics / Content / Guests / Operator), unsaved-change dots, `beforeunload` guard, `#panel` deep links, mobile-friendly horizontal nav.
- `useSiteEditor` — loads `/api/sites/:id`; `saveContent(section)` sends `expectedVersion` (409 → “reload to continue”), `saveSettings(patch)`.
- Panels, each saving only its own section: Couple & date (+ tradition), Look & sections (palette presets, “match my cover photo” AI palette, section toggles), Opening (cover photo/film + focal crop + verse), Our story, Spotlight, Monogram (generate or upload), Ceremonies (tradition presets, custom events, reorder, show/hide, **start/end time in Dhaka time, venue + Google Maps link**, banner + crop, 5-photo carousel, moments), Gallery (batch upload into albums, reorder, captions, remove), Blessings (tradition verses + parents' message), Closing & contact (opt-in public email/WhatsApp), Guestbook moderation, and operator-only Site settings (address, status, plan, products, paid-until, client record, move to Trash) and **Couple access** (create one-time invite links, copy / share on WhatsApp, remove access).
- Reuses `FocalPointPicker` and `CeremonyCarouselPicker` unchanged.

**Fixes that fall out of per-section saves**: the footer is no longer overwritten on save, duas are no longer reset to defaults, and turning a section off no longer deletes its content.

**Operator console** restyled on the UI kit (light, matches the dashboard): Overview (live/drafts/renewals in 30 days/first-50 progress, server-rendered), All sites (tabs Active/Live/Drafts/Trash with counts, search by name/address/phone, publish/unpublish, trash, restore, delete forever with confirmation), New client (names → address auto-derived, tradition, products, private client record → creates a draft pre-filled with the tradition's ceremonies and blessings, then opens the editor).

**Removed**: the 2,190-line `admin/sites/[id]/page.jsx`, the 2,109-line `admin/sites/new/page.jsx`, `PUT`/`GET /api/admin/sites/:id` and `/api/admin/messages/:id` (superseded by `/api/sites/:id/*`).
