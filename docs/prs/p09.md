# Phase 9 — Marketing site and theme demos

Base: `v2/p08-themes` · Branch: `v2/p09-marketing`

A new marketing site in English and Bangla, a filterable theme gallery, a page per theme, and 60 live demos (every theme × every tradition, as both full site and invitation). The demos use a fictional couple and openly licensed photos with no identifiable people. The old built-in demo, which used the original couple's text, is gone.

## What changed

**Marketing** (`src/marketing/*`, wording in `src/i18n/marketing.js`)
- `/` (English) and `/bn` (Bangla). The visual direction is warm paper and ink, so the colourful themes stand out (no more gold-on-black).
- Sections:
  1. Hero with three phone screenshots.
  2. Trust strip.
  3. Theme gallery filterable by tradition and mood.
  4. "One link that grows with your wedding" (invitation → the day → memories).
  5. How it works.
  6. Features: only what is built.
  7. Pricing.
  8. FAQ: privacy, downloads and screenshots stated honestly, editing, traditions, photos, payment.
  9. Footer with the BigApeWeb.com credit.
- **Pricing:** set in `PRICES`. Complete keeps the current ৳3,499; Invitation and Memories show "Ask us" until you set them. The WhatsApp number is the one from the old page.

**Themes**
- `/themes` is the full gallery. `/themes/[id]` shows desktop and phone screenshots, the description in English and Bangla, mood, the opening, palette swatches, and demo links for all five traditions (the theme's own tradition first).
- Gallery images are `public/themes/<id>/{cover,phone}.jpg`, made by `scripts/capture-theme-covers.mjs` from the demo pages. Re-run it after a theme changes, then rebuild.

**Demos** (`src/demo/*`)
- `/demo/[theme]/[tradition]` and `/demo/[theme]/[tradition]/invite`: 60 static pages, revalidated daily so their dates stay about two months ahead (countdown and RSVP stay live).
- Content is built in code from each tradition: its placeholder couple, ceremonies, verses and wording. Demos never touch the database.
- A demo bar switches theme, tradition and invitation/full site, and links to credits and pricing. The demo RSVP shows the whole flow without sending anything. Demos have no guestbook or calendar file.
- **Photos:** 17 photos from Wikimedia Commons (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA) of hands, marigolds, rings, the topor and decoration, with no faces. They are resized and stripped of metadata, in `public/demo/photos/`. Attribution is at `/demo/credits` and in `public/demo/CREDITS.md`.

**Removed**
- `lib/demo.js`, `data/{content,ceremonies,duas,demo-gallery}.json` and `scripts/seed_demo.js`. Site loading no longer has demo special cases.
- **Old URLs:** `/demo` → `/themes`, `/rafa-shawon` → `/demo/cinematic/muslim`, `/demo-hindu` → `/demo/laal-paar/hindu` (all 308).
